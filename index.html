01About

A small company built to grow

Gedz Ventures LLC is a registered limited liability company in Alaska. It's the home for the businesses we start and operate. Our first line of business is retail: sourcing genuine, brand-name products from manufacturers and authorized distributors and selling them to customers online.

We keep things simple: buy from legitimate sources, follow each brand's pricing and policies, pay on time, and communicate clearly.
